Cruel Survivor

January 2025 • 3 mo

Solo project

Tools: Miro, Google Docs

This is a creative design exercise for a AAA game, focusing on designing deep characters, human primalism, and worldbuilding. As the protagonist travels alongside his two allies with polar opposite moral values, he develops his own moral alignment. Depending on his choices, one ally will stay loyal to him, while the other turns against him and becomes the game's final boss fight.
