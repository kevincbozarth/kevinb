Music

——— Leitmotifs from DINK: The Musical ———

Becca's Theme

Becca is the main protagonist, married to Jeff. Their pregnancy revelation and abortion dilemma are major plot catalysts.

Governor Hobb's Theme

Governor Andrew Hobbs is the primary antagonist. He passes anti-abortion laws, putting Becca's life in danger.

Toxic Masculinity

Jeff hides his emotions, refusing to be vulnerable with his own wife out of compliance to "strong man" social norms.

Baby's ostinado

This ostinado represents the developing life in the womb as a result of Becca's pregnancy.

Ode To Your Womb

Perhaps the most important motif in the series, Becca and Jeff's newfound love for their upcoming baby will be stripped away.

Proudly a DINK

This melody brings the series full circle. It's the opening melody to episode one and the finale of the series.
